Adds an immense new upgrade tier for the coop and barn, holding 48 animals. Auto-compatible with SVE and multiple other coop and barn mods, and you can choose between a clean or a cozy interior!

This mod adapts to your modlist in a safe way. The mod will check for the compatible mods listed below, and change its config internally based on logic. Please note that adding or removing the compatible mods will not break this mod. The behavior that is dynamic is Robin's upgrade menu, meaning the building to be upgraded and those upgrade costs are the only things to change. If my Ultimate buildings are already present on your farm, removing compatible mods will not break those Ultimate buildings (again, only the upgrade cost and "building to upgrade" change in Robin's construction menu). Check the list below for compatible mods and the resulting behavior and see the table in the mod images for the building costs!

*A small note on map design: the player can walk behind the trough walls, but NPCs are completely blocked off from hiding back there. I have highlighted the region of the produce area, which won't allow any drops behind the walls either. Of course, the auto-petter and auto-grabber means the player isn't hunting around for NPCs to pet or drops to pick up, but I wanted to make sure these maps are as user friendly as possible. Oh yeah, you can put casks in here too!


--Compatible Mods--

N﻿ow with live config option to manually set your upgrade path! You can choose to upgrade Giga buildings or Mega buildings even with SVE installed!! (If you choose Mega, the mod will still scan for Resource Chickens and adapt)

If you want to upgrade SVE Premium buildings or the vanilla Deluxe buildings, please leave config on "Auto"!! The section below explains the behavior with "Auto" set:


Stardew Valley Expanded:
With SVE installed, it will take precedence over all the other compats, and you must upgrade the Premium Coop and Premium Barn. Both doors work on the Ultimate Barn and I've added a second door to the Ultimate Coop (see images for location) for convenience (and for fun)!

Bigger on the Inside:
You'll also need SVE to take advantage of this compatibility, and the upgrade chain will become Deluxe -> Premium -> Ultimate all while keeping the footprint and exterior appearance of the Deluxe buildings! This means your reskins will work the whole way.

Gigantic Coop and Barn:
Without SVE installed, this would be next for compatibility. You will have Deluxe building exteriors, and no extra doors. However, because these buildings are already so expensive and pretty close in capacity, I've adjusted the cost to 0 gold (also a thank you to fans of this, my other mod!!), so you only need to pay Robin materials.

Jen's Mega Coop and Barn:
This would be next down the list, and yes you can have both Mega and Resource Chickens at the same time!! Just make sure you go into the Mega config and shut off the Mega Coop so that the Giant Coop from Resource Chickens becomes available!

Resource Chickens:
If you have only this mod installed out of my compatibility list, you'll upgrade the Deluxe Barn and Giant Coop.

Otherwise, this mod works even when none of the above are installed! Just upgrade your Deluxe Coop and Barn!!

All Ultimate buildings come with a heater, auto-grabber, and auto-petter.
Ultimate Coops will always have FOUR incubators, so get crackin'!
